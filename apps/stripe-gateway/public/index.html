🛡️ How Your Bank Stays 100% Confidential

When customers pay through your Stripe integration:

  • Customers never see your bank details: Your bank account number, IFSC/routing number, and personal card numbers are never exposed.
  • Statement Descriptor: The buyer’s bank or credit card statement only shows your store name (e.g. YOUPI GLOBAL or your business brand), keeping your private bank routing completely hidden.

💳 How to Receive Direct Card Transfers (Instant Payouts)

To transfer customer funds directly onto your bank debit card:

  1. Log into your Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Payouts.
  2. Under Bank accounts and scheduling, click Add debit card.
  3. Enter your Visa or Mastercard debit card number.
  4. Enable Instant Payouts: Stripe transfers the money directly onto your card in under 30 minutes via Visa Direct / Mastercard Send rails.

🌐 International Multi-Currency Conversion
